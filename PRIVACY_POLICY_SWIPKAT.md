# Privacy Policy for SWIPKAT

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

SWIPKAT is a CAD/BIM application for Android developed and published by **Othala**. This Privacy Policy explains how SWIPKAT accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the application.

SWIPKAT is designed primarily as a local-first application. Most project data and application data remain on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses to export, share, or synchronize data with another device through SWIPKAT's local-network collaboration features.

## 1. Information SWIPKAT Does Not Collect for Advertising or Tracking

SWIPKAT does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, or similar advertising or behavioral-tracking services.

SWIPKAT does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

The current version of SWIPKAT does not request access to the device's precise or approximate location, contacts, microphone, camera, SMS messages, call logs, or advertising identifier.

## 2. Project and User-Created Data

SWIPKAT allows users to create and work with CAD/BIM information, including projects, models, families, drawings, engineering data, materials, parameters, schedules, and related files.

This content is normally stored locally on the user's device or in locations selected by the user.

Othala does not automatically upload SWIPKAT project content to an Othala-operated cloud server.

If a user exports a project or file to shared device storage or another application, that exported copy is controlled by the storage location or receiving application selected by the user.

## 3. Local Network Collaboration and Worksharing

SWIPKAT includes local-network collaboration features that can allow devices on the same LAN or Wi-Fi network to discover a SWIPKAT worksharing session and exchange project information.

When the user starts or joins a local-network worksharing session, SWIPKAT may process and exchange information such as:

- project name and project identifier;
- project snapshots, revisions, and project changes;
- participant identifier;
- participant display name;
- device name;
- worksharing activity and revision information;
- element borrowing or lease information; and
- technical session identifiers required to maintain the local collaboration session.

This information is exchanged with participating devices on the local network for the purpose of providing collaboration and synchronization features. The current version does not route this worksharing data through an Othala-operated cloud service.

Users should use local collaboration only on networks and with devices they trust. Information intentionally shared with another participating device may remain on that device after the collaboration session ends.

## 4. Google Play Billing

SWIPKAT uses the Google Play Billing Library to support purchases or subscriptions that may be offered through Google Play.

Google Play processes payment information. SWIPKAT does not directly receive or store full credit-card or debit-card numbers.

To provide and verify purchased features, SWIPKAT may receive and process purchase-related metadata from Google Play, including:

- product identifier;
- purchase status;
- purchase token;
- Google Play order identifier, when available;
- acknowledgement status; and
- purchased quantity.

This information is used only for billing-related functionality, purchase verification, entitlement handling, and restoration of purchases.

Payment transactions and Google Play account information are also subject to Google's own privacy practices and terms.

## 5. Diagnostic and Crash Information

SWIPKAT includes an on-device diagnostic system used to help identify stability, rendering, memory, and crash problems.

Diagnostic information may include:

- device manufacturer and model;
- Android version and API level;
- application process-exit information;
- exception type, message, and stack trace;
- memory usage;
- rendering and performance metrics;
- operation names, object identifiers, counts, and technical events required for debugging.

The diagnostic system is designed not to serialize the contents of the user's BIM model into its crash log.

Diagnostic and crash reports are stored locally in the application's private storage. SWIPKAT does not automatically transmit these reports to Othala. If a user voluntarily exports or sends a diagnostic report for support, the information becomes part of that support communication.

## 6. Device and Network Information

SWIPKAT may access basic network state, Wi-Fi state, and local-network connectivity as necessary for features such as Google Play Billing and local worksharing.

These permissions are used to provide application functionality and are not used to determine the user's geographic location.

## 7. Application Settings and Local Storage

SWIPKAT may store application preferences and operational state locally, including language, interface preferences, project state, drawings, temporary data, diagnostic state, and other settings necessary for the application to function.

Release builds disable Android application backup for SWIPKAT's private application data. However, files that a user explicitly exports to a shared or user-selected location are outside SWIPKAT's private application storage and may be subject to the backup or synchronization behavior of that location or another application.

## 8. Accounts

The current version of SWIPKAT does not require users to create an online SWIPKAT account with Othala.

Local worksharing participant identities are used for collaboration within the relevant worksharing session and are not equivalent to an online SWIPKAT account.

If an online account system is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that functionality becomes available.

## 9. How Information Is Shared

SWIPKAT does not sell or rent user data.

Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

- **At the user's direction:** for example, when the user exports a file, shares a project, joins a local worksharing session, or sends a diagnostic report.
- **With Google Play:** as required to provide Google Play Billing and purchase-related functionality.
- **For legal reasons:** if disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.
- **To protect users or the service:** when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, security problems, abuse, or threats to the rights or safety of users, Othala, or others.

## 10. Data Security

SWIPKAT is designed to minimize unnecessary data transmission and to keep core project data local whenever possible.

Private application files and diagnostic logs are stored using Android application-private storage where applicable. Release builds disable Android backup of private application data.

Local-network collaboration communicates between participating devices on the user's network. Users should not use worksharing over public or untrusted networks and should control which devices are permitted to participate.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local SWIPKAT data is generally retained on the user's device until the user deletes the relevant project or file, clears SWIPKAT's application data, or uninstalls the application.

Uninstalling SWIPKAT or clearing its application data may remove data stored in SWIPKAT's private application storage. Files that were exported to shared storage or another user-selected location may remain there and must be deleted separately by the user.

Information transferred to another device through local worksharing may remain on that device until it is deleted there.

Purchase records handled by Google Play may be retained by Google in accordance with Google's applicable policies and legal obligations.

Because the current version does not maintain an Othala-hosted user account or cloud project repository, Othala generally does not possess a server-side copy of users' local project data to delete.

## 12. User Choices and Privacy Rights

Users can control their SWIPKAT data by managing local project files, exported files, local worksharing sessions, application permissions, and Android application storage.

Depending on the user's jurisdiction, additional privacy rights may apply. Privacy questions or requests concerning information actually held by Othala may be submitted using the contact information below.

## 13. Children's Privacy

SWIPKAT is a professional CAD/BIM and engineering application and is not specifically directed to children.

SWIPKAT does not knowingly use advertising or behavioral-tracking systems to profile children. If Othala becomes aware that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, appropriate steps will be taken to address it.

## 14. International Use

SWIPKAT may be made available in multiple countries and languages. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of project data, collaboration features, and exported information complies with any laws, confidentiality duties, or contractual requirements applicable to them.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SWIPKAT's functionality, data practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

When material changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be revised. The current version of the policy will be made available through the application's official privacy-policy location.
